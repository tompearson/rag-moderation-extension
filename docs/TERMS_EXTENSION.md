## What the service provides

The extension offers a **side-panel tool** to submit text you choose and receive **AI-assisted moderation suggestions**, optionally backed by retrieved guidelines (RAG). We may provide a **limited number of free moderations** on shared capacity when you are not using your own Google Gemini API key. Features, limits, and availability may change without notice.

**The extension does not replace your judgment, your platform’s official tools, or legal advice.** You are responsible for final moderation decisions and for complying with your community’s policies.
